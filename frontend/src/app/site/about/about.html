A family photography studio in Winter Haven, Florida

Memories Made Visible is run by Erica and Austin Cuddeback from our home base in Winter Haven, and we travel anywhere in Polk County — Lakeland, Bartow, Auburndale, Haines City, Lake Wales and beyond. We photograph the moments people want to hold on to — weddings, new babies, graduations, and the ordinary days that turn out to matter most.

Lead photographer

Erica Cuddeback

Erica leads every session, from the first planning conversation to the final edit. Her approach is calm and well-directed: she handles the posing and the light so you can focus on each other.

She photographs weddings, families, couples, maternity and newborn sessions, seniors and events throughout Polk County.

Second photographer & operations

Austin Cuddeback

Austin shoots alongside Erica at weddings and events, covering the second angle so nothing is missed. He also runs the studio's operations — scheduling, contracts and gallery delivery.
